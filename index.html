01 / About

A bit about me.

How I work

Understand the problem. Build something small. Learn from how people use it.

I like working on the whole product: talking to the people who'll use it, deciding what to build, and getting a first version in front of them.

Lately most of what I build is for small businesses and teams: truck repair shops, customs brokers, restaurants, a catering company, and a local florist and café.

Before focusing on product, I spent years as a software engineer on TELMEX billing and sales systems, and on the database engine and developer portal at Teradata. Lately a lot of my work involves AI, but I pick the tool based on the problem.
